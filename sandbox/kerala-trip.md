# 5-Day Trip to Kerala

## Itinerary

### Day 1: Arrival in Kochi
- **Morning:** Arrive at Kochi International Airport.
- **Afternoon:** Check-in at hotel. Visit Fort Kochi and explore the Chinese Fishing Nets.
- **Evening:** Enjoy sunset at Marine Drive followed by a traditional Kerala dinner.

### Day 2: Munnar
- **Morning:** Travel to Munnar (approx. 4-hour drive).
- **Afternoon:** Visit the Tea Museum and explore tea plantations.
- **Evening:** Scenic views from Top Station.

### Day 3: Thekkady
- **Morning:** Depart for Thekkady (approx. 3-hour drive).
- **Afternoon:** Go for a spice plantation tour.
- **Evening:** Enjoy a boat ride on Periyar Lake.

### Day 4: Alleppey
- **Morning:** Travel to Alleppey (approx. 4-hour drive).
- **Afternoon:** Check-in to a houseboat and explore the backwaters.
- **Evening:** Dinner on the houseboat with local cuisine.

### Day 5: Departure from Kochi
- **Morning:** Disembark from the houseboat, head back to Kochi.
- **Afternoon:** Last minute shopping and sightseeing.
- **Evening:** Depart from Kochi International Airport.

## Estimated Budget
- **Accommodation:** $50 per night x 4 nights = $200
- **Food:** $25 per day x 5 days = $125
- **Transportation:** $100 (within Kerala)
- **Activities and Entrance Fees:** $75
- **Total Estimated Budget:** **$600**

## Packing Checklist
- **Clothing:** Light clothing suitable for warm weather, swimwear, a light jacket for cooler nights.
- **Footwear:** Comfortable shoes for walking, sandals for beach and boat rides.
- **Toiletries:** Sunscreen, insect repellent, personal hygiene items.
- **Electronics:** Camera, power bank, charger, travel adapter.
- **Miscellaneous:** Water bottle, first-aid kit, travel documents, cash.
