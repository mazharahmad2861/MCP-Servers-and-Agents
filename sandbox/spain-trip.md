# 5-Day Trip to Spain

## Overview
This itinerary covers some of the best cities in Spain, including Barcelona, Madrid, and Seville. It details daily activities, culinary experiences, and adventure opportunities.

---

## Day 1: Arrival in Barcelona
- **Morning:**  
  - Arrive in Barcelona. 
  - Check into your hotel and freshen up.

- **Afternoon:**  
  - Visit the iconic **Sagrada Familia**.  
  - Take a stroll down **Passeig de Gràcia** to see **Casa Batlló** and **Casa Milà**.

- **Evening:**  
  - Dinner at a local tapas bar. **Cuisine:** Try patatas bravas, jamón ibérico, and seafood tapas.
  - Explore the **Barri Gòtic** (Gothic Quarter) at night.

---

## Day 2: More of Barcelona
- **Morning:**  
  - Visit **Park Güell** and enjoy the colorful architecture.  
  - Take a leisurely walk along **La Rambla**. 

- **Afternoon:**  
  - Head to **Montjuïc** hill for a panoramic view of the city.  
  - Visit the **Magic Fountain**.

- **Evening:**  
  - Dinner in the **El Born** district. **Cuisine:** Enjoy seafood paella and churros with chocolate.
  - Attend a **flamenco show**.

---

## Day 3: Travel to Madrid
- **Morning:**  
  - Take a high-speed train to Madrid (approx. 3 hours). 
  - Check into your hotel.

- **Afternoon:**  
  - Visit **Prado Museum** and explore its rich art collection.  
  - Stroll through **Retiro Park**.

- **Evening:**  
  - Dinner at **Mercado de San Miguel**. **Cuisine:** Sample various Spanish delicacies.
  - Explore the **Plaza Mayor**.

---

## Day 4: Discovering Madrid
- **Morning:**  
  - Visit **Royal Palace of Madrid**.  
  - Explore the **Gran Via** area.

- **Afternoon:**  
  - Visit the **Thyssen-Bornemisza Museum**.  
  - Walk through **Chueca** neighborhood.

- **Evening:**  
  - Dinner at a local restaurant. **Cuisine:** Try cocido madrileño (chickpea stew).
  - Enjoy nightlife in **Malasaña**.

---

## Day 5: Travel to Seville
- **Morning:**  
  - Take a train to Seville (approx. 2.5 hours). 
  - Check into your hotel.

- **Afternoon:**  
  - Visit the **Seville Cathedral** and **La Giralda**.  
  - Explore the beautiful **Alcázar of Seville**.

- **Evening:**  
  - Dinner at a traditional Andalusian restaurant. **Cuisine:** Sample gazpacho and flamenquín.
  - Take a walk in the **Santa Cruz** neighborhood.

---

## Additional Tips:
- **Transportation:**  
  - Use local metro systems, buses, and trains for easy navigation.

- **Cultural Etiquette:**  
  - Dinner is generally served late (around 9 PM). 
  - Tipping is not customary but appreciated.

Enjoy your trip to Spain!
